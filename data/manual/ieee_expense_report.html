IEEE Expense Report: NextGen Expense Reimbursement (Concur) - How to Get Reimbursed

This page explains how IEEE volunteers get reimbursed for expenses they pay on behalf of IEEE, including expenses for a Section event. Reimbursement is handled through the NextGen Expense Reimbursement tool, which is powered by SAP Concur. IEEE reimburses you directly. Source: IEEE Corporate Activities, IEEE Expense Report page (corporate.ieee.org/resources/travel-medical-and-insurance/ieee-expense-report).

How to get reimbursed - steps to get started

Step 1 - Get started. Request an IEEE Account if you do not already have one (an IEEE username and password are required to access the platform). Then create your Expense Reimbursement account (Concur registration).

Step 2 - Access / log in. Log in to the NextGen Expense Reimbursement tool. The tool uses a single sign-on process through ieee.org/expense (also reachable via ieee.org/concur), NOT through concur.com. Logging in may require an IEEE Network login. All users need an IEEE account to access the platform.

Step 3 - Download the app (optional). You can use the Concur mobile app to create, submit, and approve expense reports anytime and anywhere, including uploading images of receipts on the go.

To submit a reimbursement: create an expense report in NextGen Expense (Concur), enter the expenses (for example, the cost you paid for a Section event), attach images of your receipts, select the appropriate Expense Report Purpose (ERP), and submit the report for approval. Reimbursement is paid electronically.

Checklist and help

For step-by-step help getting started and submitting, refer to the NextGen Expense Reimbursement Checklist (PDF). For general questions, contact IEEE's NextGen Expense project team at nextgenexpense@ieee.org. For technical assistance, contact SAP Concur's user support desk. See also the Concur Frequently Asked Questions (PDF).

Benefits of using your Expense Reimbursement account

Receive payments faster with electronic payment processing. Single process for submitting expense reimbursement requests. Track the status of expense reports in real time. Autofill and capture credit card transaction details securely. Upload images of receipts at any time. Improved processing of international payments and reporting. Integration with third-party applications such as Uber. Store banking information on a secure platform for faster electronic reimbursement.

Training and quick reference

Available training and quick-reference materials include: an Overview of Concur slide deck; the NextGen Expense new user interface walkthrough; a recorded demo and self-paced video tutorial; recorded training for Sections and Standards Working Groups; GST/VAT requirements for Canadian residents; Navigating NextGen Expense (Concur); Tips and Tricks for Faster Payment; Exception Icons and Definitions for Travel and Expense; and the Expense Report Purpose (ERP) library (used for selecting the ERP, approver workflows, and GL coding).

Important notes

The NextGen Expense Reimbursement tool is an automated platform designed to be fast, easy, and efficient, and is part of the IEEE NextGen platform. Log in through ieee.org/expense using IEEE single sign-on, not concur.com. An IEEE account is required.
